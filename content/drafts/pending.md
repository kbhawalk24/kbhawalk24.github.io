## /work/finding-data · What I would do differently

I would have run the usability study I planned in 2022 instead of testing one feature at a time; the inconsistencies my manager noted that year would have surfaced sooner. Before the 2025 rebuild I would have given exact-name search its own measure, because it got worse while everything else improved. ## /work/building-the-platform · What I would do differently

In the first year I traded consistency for speed, and the design system paid for it later. In 2024 I took on too many parallel projects; my own review that year says so. And on onboarding, I would draw every state and edge case before the second design went to engineering, not after; it is the drawing that found the cases that stalled it.

## /work/my-teams-work · What I would do differently

I would have started the shared research base in my first month as a manager instead of my second year; it is what finally made design's case in roadmap terms. And I would have asked for a decision-maker by name on every project whose research we were about to run, so that findings had somewhere to land.
