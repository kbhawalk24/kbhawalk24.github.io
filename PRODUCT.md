# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, design leaders and recruiters filling Principal or Staff product design roles and design manager roles at data-platform, developer-tool and AI companies (confirmed 11 Nov 2026; both tracks equal). They skim: a first pass of a minute or two on the homepage, then one case study read properly if the first pass lands. Some read on a phone from a recruiter's message.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio of Kanchi Bhawalkar, a product design leader with 12+ years shaping developer platforms, agentic AI and enterprise data systems. Success is a reader who books a conversation: the homepage earns the click into a case study, and a case study earns the email.

## Positioning

In the first minute a reader should conclude that she designs complex data and AI tools that engineers trust, end to end, with a former backend engineer's depth. The record behind it: founding designer of Intuit's data platform (2021 to 2026), designer or design director of all seven of its product areas, then manager of its design team (Aug 2024 to Jul 2026); before that the first designer at 605 (2017 to 2021).

## Operating Context

- A static Next.js site deployed to kbhawalk24.github.io on every push to `main`; the resume PDF, LinkedIn and email are the next steps a reader takes.
- Four case studies (Search 2.0, the data agent, the catalog-to-platform story, 605), a Leadership page, a Timeline page.
- Umami Cloud records page views and tagged clicks; every clickable element carries an event (see CLAUDE.md).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content rules in CLAUDE.md are binding: colleagues credited by role, never by name; client names only inside case studies; figures only when sourced (`docs/`, gitignored `private/`) and consistent across pages; the eight Intuit product areas always listed in full; "Every designer who reported to me was promoted" stays as that sentence; no em-dashes in site copy; plain, factual headings; quotes only where they carry a fact.
- The repo is public. Job-search notes, review quotes and target lists live in `private/`, never in tracked files.
- Every new link, button or card gets an analytics event when it is written.

## Brand Commitments

- Her name, her headshot in the hero (`public/images/kanchi.jpeg`, since 11 Nov 2026; the hand-drawn illustration `public/images/eye-frames/c.webp` is retired), and the voice of the current copy: first person, plain, specific, no sales language.
- Title line: "Product Design Manager and Principal Designer".

## Evidence on Hand

- Case studies with sourced figures: Search 2.0 (first-result precision 81.31% to 89.24%), the data agent (question to verified answer 45 min to 4 min), the platform (1,886 to 6,673 monthly users, Jul 2023 to Mar 2026, across 320,000 tables), 605 ($2M additional booked revenue).
- Leadership: three product designers, about 550 engineers and 16 product managers (1 : 85), 24 launches 2024 to 2026, every report promoted.
- Prototype clips and screenshots under `public/work/`; the resume at `public/kanchi-bhawalkar-resume.pdf`.
- No testimonials are cleared for the homepage; unsourced figures (listed in `private/`) must not appear.

## Product Principles

1. Prove it with the work: the artefact and the sourced number lead, adjectives follow.
2. Show both tracks at full weight: the IC craft and the team leadership are equal evidence.
3. Respect a skim: what a reader needs is findable in seconds, and depth is one click away.
4. Never inflate: role, scope and figures stay exactly as sourced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard reachable, visible focus, 44px touch targets, WCAG AA contrast, motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
