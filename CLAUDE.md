## Analytics: keep every click tracked

Umami Cloud is the analytics. The tracker is `components/analytics.tsx`, rendered once in `app/layout.tsx`; it loads only in production builds and only when `UMAMI_WEBSITE_ID` in `lib/analytics.ts` is set. Page views are automatic. Clicks are recorded through `data-umami-event` attributes, produced by the `event()` helper in `lib/analytics.ts`.

Rules, applied on every change and checked before every push:

1. **Never remove or bypass the tracker.** `app/layout.tsx` keeps `<Analytics />`; `lib/analytics.ts` keeps the website ID `60d41913-e7f0-4512-b462-42c3be1bf8e4`. Do not add a second analytics tool.
2. **Every new clickable element gets an event.** Any link, button or card added to the site is tagged with `{...event('<name>', { ...detail })}` when it is written, not later. Names in use: `work-card` (detail `card`, `via`: panel, title or read, or wheel, the "Read the case study" link under a project still in the wheel Overview option), `hero-button` (detail `button`: work, management, resume or linkedin), `nav` (detail `item`), `next-case` (detail `from`), `wheel` (detail `button`: play, pause or replay, on the lifecycle wheel), `contact` (detail `link`: email, resume or linkedin; on the homepage the two Overview buttons, resume and linkedin, and on case study pages the footer), `leadership` (detail `via`: home, the Management section's link on the experimental pages, or ai, the Leveraging AI tab's link; both open `/#management` since /leadership was removed on 16 Nov 2026), `management` (detail `item`: the node clicked or focused in the homepage Management lineage graph, e.g. `node-fig-clean`, or story-situation, story-visible, story-upstream, story-practices, story-understood or story-impact, the Contents card on the Management tab). `clip` (detail `card` and `action`: play or pause, the preview button on a work card's clip; on the band and tour Overview options the card is overview-<clip>, overview-<case study slug> for a project still in the wheel, or overview-tour). `overview-card` (detail `card`: search-lineage, data-map or data-management), a screen capture in the fan Overview option, selected to bring it to the front. `talk` (detail `talk`: reinvent-2025 or investor-day-2023), the homepage talks row's links to the YouTube videos; the videos are no longer embedded in the case studies (12 Nov 2026). `quick-link` (detail `link`: ai, agents or player-coach, and `via`: query, the row in the Overview's terminal window that selects a question, or card, the preview card beside it that opens the case study), the three questions under "queries people run on this profile" on the catalog Overview. The three questions are off the page since 15 Nov 2026 (`CatalogQueries` in `components/catalog-hero.tsx` is kept but not rendered), so no `quick-link` event is in the HTML until they return. `overdrive` (detail `page`: index, search, lineage, film, hero, catalog or annotated, and `item`; on the annotated page home, nav-design-work, nav-management, nav-leveraging-ai, nav-talks, resume, hero-work, hero-management, leadership, or clip-search-lineage, clip-data-map, clip-data-management and clip-data-copilot (the labels around its hero clips; the Play/Pause button under them uses `clip` with card annotated-<clip>), while its case study cards use `work-card` and its footer `contact`; on the hero pages the switcher's develop, cursor, thread or live; (the catalog pages and their switcher were removed on 16 Nov 2026)), every interactive element on the experimental /overdrive pages. Reuse these names for the same kinds of element; add a new kebab-case name only for a new kind, and list it here.
3. **New pages need nothing extra** for page views, but their outbound links (resume, LinkedIn, external sites, prototypes) get an event.
4. **Before pushing, verify the attributes survived.** Run `pnpm build` and check that `out/index.html` still contains `data-umami-event="work-card"`, `"contact"` and `"nav"` (the homepage is the catalog page since 14 Nov 2026; `hero-button` now lives only on the /overdrive/hero pages), and that the page you changed contains the events you added. After the deploy, the live HTML at kbhawalk24.github.io must contain `cloud.umami.is/script.js`.
5. **Local never counts.** The tracker stays gated on `process.env.NODE_ENV === 'production'`, so the dev server, scratchpad renders and screenshots are never recorded.

## Layout rules

- **Everything spans end to end.** No heading and no paragraph, on any page, gets a max-width or `text-balance`; text runs the full width of its column and wraps where the column ends. No reading-measure caps.
- **Opening facts are a four-column table of short lines.** Under the hero of every case page: Role, Scope, Research, Shipped (the removed Management page used Systems and Outcomes for the last two), each as stacked lines of a few words with the date or count set lighter in brackets (`PageFact.lines`). Never a sentence per cell.
